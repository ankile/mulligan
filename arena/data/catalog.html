Derived views select episodes from their parents; episode-lineage-audit.json maps each view episode to its parent episode.

1. Original recordings

Mixed teleop and DAgger sessions preserve every recorded attempt. Evaluation parents preserve their policy roster and shared starts.

2. Training and validation views

Views select episodes from parents. They are useful inputs, but adding their episode counts would count recordings twice.

3. Model-round evaluations

Evaluation blocks stay distinct. An earlier evaluation can feed a later critic; consult the recipe before treating it as held out.

How to read rounds and names

cNN is a collection increment; rNN is an evaluated model round; bNN is a recorded evaluation block. All policy rollout datasets end in policy-rollouts.

Routing maps C00 → R0, C01+C02 → R1, C03+C04 → R2, C05+C06 → R3, C07+C08 → R4, and C09 → R5. Its final R0–R5 evaluation is one 15-policy repository. The velocity-action collector evaluations that the R5 critic trained on keep their original model round as velocity-rNN.

Mainline contains the primary collections and selected evaluations. Supporting contains policy rollouts and their source recordings. Ablation contains same-campaign no-CF, Sobol, and first100 alternatives.
